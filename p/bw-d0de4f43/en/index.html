B&W Križ

Modern Comfort in Medieval Vipavski Križ

B&W Križ forms part of the historic defensive walls of Vipavski Križ, one of Slovenia's best-preserved medieval settlements. In the heart of the Vipava Valley, it offers peaceful and comfortable accommodation for business travellers and guests seeking rest and a break from the bustle of everyday life.

Our carefully restored historic building combines the character of the past with contemporary architecture and comfort. Seven modern rooms, featuring a distinctive combination of concrete, black metal and wood, provide a welcoming setting for a relaxing stay.

Start your day with breakfast prepared mainly from locally sourced ingredients, enjoy a quiet moment by the fireplace, or admire the views across the Vipava Valley from our terrace.

Just a few steps from our doorstep, narrow stone streets invite you to explore Vipavski Križ, while the surrounding countryside offers opportunities to discover local cuisine, wine, nature and a rich cultural heritage.

B&W Križ is a place where past and present come together naturally. We invite you to take some time for yourself and experience the Vipava Valley at your own pace.

Vipavski Križ and Surroundings

Vipavski Križ

Vipavski Križ is much more than a picturesque medieval village. Its history dates back to pre-Roman times, and it was first mentioned in written records in 1252 as Villa Crucis. At the end of the 15th century, the settlement was fortified with defensive walls. It was granted market rights in 1507 and town status in 1532.

Today, its narrow stone streets, castle ruins, historic walls and Capuchin monastery bear witness to its rich past. The monastery was once home to the renowned preacher Janez Svetokriški. A walk through Vipavski Križ is therefore also a journey through centuries of history.

Vipavski Križ is also an excellent starting point for discovering the Vipava Valley, its cuisine, winemaking traditions, and natural and cultural attractions.

Location – A Base for Exploring

B&W Križ is located in the Vipava Valley, where different landscapes and cultural influences meet. From here, you can explore vineyard-covered hills, villages on the Karst plateau, natural attractions and the rich heritage of western Slovenia.

Our location is also an excellent base for longer day trips. Ljubljana, Lake Bled, the Soča Valley, the Slovenian coast and neighbouring Italy offer plenty to discover, while the peaceful pace of life in Vipavski Križ awaits you at the end of the day.

About Us

About Us

Architecture – Where Past Meets Present

When renovating B&W Križ, architect Vladimir Slamič drew inspiration from the building's history and its place within the medieval defensive walls. His aim was to preserve the original character and simplicity of the former agricultural building, reflecting the way people once lived, while highlighting these qualities through carefully considered contemporary architectural elements.

The renovation also included the interior design. One particularly distinctive feature is the open central staircase connecting the shared lounge with the guest rooms upstairs. Its new position also opened up improved views towards Čaven and the Gora plateau.

Architecture and interior design: Vladimir Slamič, architect.

Wine as Part of Our Culture

The name B&W Križ comes from the connection between accommodation and wine – Bed & Wine. Today, we see wine primarily as part of the Vipava Valley's cultural landscape: a product of knowledge, hard work, tradition and human connections.

We believe that understanding something helps us appreciate it more. That is why we are happy to introduce our guests to local winemakers, where they can discover their stories, vineyards and wines. Wine is part of the region's identity, but it is by no means essential to enjoying a stay with us.

What Matters to Us

We believe that genuine hospitality does not need grand words. What matters is a warm welcome, respect for privacy and attention to the small details that make guests feel comfortable.

We value simplicity and authenticity. When renovating the house, we wanted to preserve its historic character while adapting it to contemporary living. We give preference to local suppliers when choosing ingredients and make an effort to reduce waste and unnecessary packaging in our everyday activities.

We can communicate with our guests in Slovenian, English, Italian, German and Croatian.

Above all, we want our guests to feel at ease and discover Vipavski Križ as it truly is.

Who We Are

B&W Križ grew out of our connection to Vipavski Križ, its history and the winemaking traditions of the Vipava Valley.

Tjaša works professionally in quality management and has long been interested in wine, local heritage and development projects. These interests and experiences also shape the way we welcome guests and develop B&W Križ.

Borja is an artist specialising in historical reenactment, particularly bringing the past to life through costumes, objects and demonstrations of everyday life in earlier times. His work creates another connection between our family and the historic setting of Vipavski Križ.

B&W Križ is therefore more than accommodation in a historic building. It is also a place where different interests and a shared appreciation of the place we call home come together.
